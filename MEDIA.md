## What was deliberately left out

**Personal data.** The vehicle registration plate in the tracking footage is blurred in both the still and the clip. Web-development layout screenshots were excluded because a student identity card and a bank card were visible in a background window. Internship records, job-application material, and project photographs showing identifiable people are not published.

**Third-party material.** The Computer Networks folder contains roughly nine hours of recorded lectures built on another author's slides and showing the lecturer on camera; none of it is republished here. Vendor product imagery and the demo photographs bundled with third-party research code were also excluded, as neither is this archive's work.

**Size.** Several recordings in the source folders exceed GitHub's 100 MB single-file limit. The clips that are included were re-encoded to a web-friendly size; the full-resolution originals, along with datasets, model weights, VM images, and archives, remain only in the local course folders.
